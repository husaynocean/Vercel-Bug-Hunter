Sample finding

What a finding looks like

Reproduced on the public PoolTogether contest (Code4rena, March 2024), where this bug was a confirmed High. Shortened from the full report.

High · Accounting

A partial fee claim wipes the whole fee balance, so the unclaimed fees are lost

PrizeVault.sol · claimYieldFeeShares() · L611–L622

Root cause. The function subtracts the full cached balance instead of the amount claimed. Whenever the fee recipient claims less than everything, the balance drops to zero but only the claimed shares are minted. The rest is no longer owed to anyone.

Impact. The fee recipient permanently loses the unclaimed part of its fees on every partial claim, and those assets get swept into the prize pool as yield. It happens in normal use, with no attacker.

// test: claim half the fees, then try to claim the rest
vm.prank(bob);
vault.claimYieldFeeShares(half);
assertEq(vault.balanceOf(bob), half);
assertEq(vault.yieldFeeBalance(), 0);   // whole balance wiped

vm.prank(bob);
vm.expectRevert(/* SharesExceedsYieldFeeBalance(rest, 0) */);
vault.claimYieldFeeShares(rest);        // the rest is gone
// fix
-        yieldFeeBalance -= _yieldFeeBalance;
+        yieldFeeBalance -= _shares;

The test passes on the audited commit and fails once the fix is applied.
